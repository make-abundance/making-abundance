# Abundance is Here Now

Notice your shoulders. Right now. Are they up? Is your jaw tight? Is there a low hum of vigilance running beneath everything — scanning for what might go wrong, who might need managing, whether you're doing enough to deserve what you have?

That's not stress. That's not "just how life is." That's the effort of maintaining scarcity. You've been doing it all day, every day, for years. The bracing for impact that never comes. The performance of earning your right to exist. The quiet dread that if you stop pushing, everything falls apart.

It's exhausting. And it's optional.

What if you set it down? Not forever — just for a breath. Let the shoulders drop. Let the scanning stop. Notice what's already here when you stop working so hard to protect it: people around you, resilience you forgot you had, enough. Not a future to build toward. Something already present, obscured only by the effort of looking elsewhere.

That's abundance. Not a theory. Not a policy proposal. The thing that's already here when you stop maintaining the fiction that it isn't.

## Where to go from here

This is a living exploration — some pieces are well-developed, some are open questions waiting for the right person to walk in. The open questions are invitations, not gaps.

**[[The Contraction|I want to feel this.]]**
Start with the contraction — the body-level experience of maintaining scarcity. Name it, recognize it, begin to set it down. This is the front door.

**[[The Argument Clarified|I want to understand this.]]**
Start with the structural case — how the cycle of abundance is already complete, why the only remaining constraint is organizational, and what governance makes extraction impossible.

**[[The Campfire|I want to do this with a group.]]**
Start with the experience — a 90-minute SI-facilitated session where a group of people feel what it's like to make decisions without the contraction. *(This is a design — not yet tested.)*
